✦ Our Story

The Pocket Behind Yojigen Poketto

A small Dhaka-based shop for anyone who believes their keys deserve a little personality.

How It Started

Yojigen Poketto — "four-dimensional pocket" — began the way most good ideas do: with too many keyrings and not enough places to put them. What started as a small shoebox of hand-picked charms for friends slowly turned into orders from strangers, then from other cities, then from every corner of Bangladesh.

We're anime fans, cartoon fans, gamers, and certified fans of anything with a cute face. Every charm we sell is something we'd genuinely clip onto our own bag first.
